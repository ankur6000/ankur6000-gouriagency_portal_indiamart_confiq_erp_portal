# Third-party data notices

## India 2026 regional holiday data

The regional holiday snapshot in `src/indiaPublicHolidays2026.json` is compiled by IndiaDigital from the Reserve Bank of India regional holiday matrix. IndiaDigital provides the dataset under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0). Attribution: IndiaDigital, <https://www.indiadigital.org/open/data/>. Primary source: RBI, <https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/HolidayMatrixDisplay.aspx>.

The app uses exact RBI regional lists where available and state/union-territory administration notices where an RBI regional list is unavailable or was mapped approximately. Administrative amendments are included where verified. Religious dates and local closures may still be amended by the issuing authority.
